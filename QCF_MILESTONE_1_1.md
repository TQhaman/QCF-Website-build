# QCF Milestone 1.1 — first-slice refinement

This refinement responds to the first rendered review.

## Changes made

- Replaced corporate-sounding `enterprise` in hero/SEO copy with more human, concrete festival language.
- Hero strip is now `STREET / CULTURE / VOICE / PLACE`.
- The green accent moved with `VOICE`; `PLACE` is quieter and neutral.
- Reworked Festival Story copy to sound client-facing rather than like internal design notes.
- `Culture` now explicitly makes room for music, food, fashion/runway, visual art, design and storytelling.
- Replaced `Community` with `Voice` so the story sequence matches the hero strip and can still carry artists, residents, visitors, vendors and local creatives.
- Reworked `Place` around Caxton & Burns Streets / the street precinct; removed coast-first wording.
- Updated the Place media placeholder to request drone/street-precinct photography.
- Humanised the Editions section and removed internal strategy language from visible copy.
- Added a contact-data scaffold in `festivalConfig`; values remain null until the QCF team confirms official email, ticket and social links.
